Source: [3DAssets.dev Dental Practice and Surgery](https://3dassets.dev/packs/dental-practice-and-surgery)

Source page identifies the pack as **CC0 1.0 Universal**. The repository preserves the original source manifest and per-model download index, plus the complete 54-GLB source batch. The assembled starter scene is included in that batch as `Dental practice floor (Dental Practice and Surgery).glb` (source asset 34423); it is not duplicated under another filename. No geometry was modified in this copy. Attribution is not required under CC0; source credit is retained for provenance.

This is a clinic environment/equipment set, not a complete interactive training app or a clinically validated anatomy set. The models provide room, furniture and equipment geometry; the app must provide navigation, procedure logic, interaction and any required colliders.
